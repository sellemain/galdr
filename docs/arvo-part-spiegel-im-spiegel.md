The piece begins almost before it has made a claim. A quiet tone appears, then another, and the room has to lower itself to meet them. There is no beat to take the body. There is only placement: a line entering, a harmonic ground answering, each sound leaving enough space around itself that the next one arrives exposed.

The first minute teaches the scale. The music does not ask for attention by moving quickly. It makes attention smaller and more exact. The upper line steps through the air with almost no excess weight, while the lower part keeps a warm, plain floor underneath it. The pulse is felt as interval and return rather than percussion. Time is counted by arrivals.

By 1:00, the pattern is already trustworthy, but trust does not make it passive. Every repetition has to survive its own bareness. The melody rises and falls in narrow arcs, then the harmony changes the color around it by a few degrees. Those small changes matter because there is nowhere for them to hide. A warmer interval opens, a darker one passes through, and the whole room tilts without becoming dramatic.

Around 2:00, the piece has settled into its discipline. The high line seems to look at the same center from another distance. The lower notes do not push it forward; they receive it. That is the main pressure here: not force, but patience held so steadily that the ear starts to feel responsible for every decay. I notice the ends of tones as much as their beginnings. The music makes listening wait until a sound has fully left.

Near 3:00, the repeated motion begins to feel less like a figure and more like a way of breathing. The rises are modest. The releases are modest. Nothing breaks the surface, but the surface keeps changing. The harmony carries a soft major light, then lets a minor shade pass across it without announcing a new scene. The effect is not sadness exactly. It is attention passing through a narrow doorway and finding the same room altered.

The middle span is where the severity becomes clearest. Around 4:00, the piece is still doing what it has been doing, and that continuity becomes the event. There is no decorative filling-in, no rescue by density. The line continues to place itself against the ground, and the ground continues to hold. The music has a strong pattern, but it is not locked in the body. It is locked in proportion. Each return is exact enough to make a slight harmonic turn feel large.

By 5:00, the listener has stopped waiting for a larger arrival. The piece has replaced arrival with care. The upper line climbs, pauses, and descends as if the important thing is not direction but the honesty of each step. The resonance around the notes is thin enough to hear the room inside the recording. There is warmth, but it is not lush. It is warmth with discipline, warmth that refuses to blur the edges.

Around 6:15, the harmonic field opens briefly with more brightness, and because the texture is so spare, that small opening feels spacious. The music does not swell into it. It lets the brightness stand, then moves on. This is how the piece develops: not by adding material, but by changing what the same material can bear. A turn toward shadow a moment later feels equally large, not because it is loud, but because the ear has become calibrated to tiny pressure changes.

The seventh minute deepens the hold. The melody keeps returning to the center and leaving again. The lower part remains steady enough to feel almost architectural, but it never becomes a wall. It is a frame. The upper line passes through it with the same plainness each time, and the plainness starts to feel demanding. There is no theatrical emphasis to tell the listener what matters. Placement tells you.

By 8:00, the piece begins to feel as if it is preserving less force for each outward motion. The pattern continues, but the energy is thinner. The line still rises, still answers itself, still returns, yet the space between gestures carries more of the weight. The late phrases do not announce an ending; they make continuation feel increasingly delicate. The music is still exact, but its exactness is now closer to disappearance.

Around 9:25, the hold loosens. The pattern that has carried the piece for so long begins to lose its forward certainty, and the small breaks near the close register sharply because almost nothing else has broken. The final tones do not try to gather the whole piece into a last statement. They keep the same modest authority the opening had. A note is placed, allowed to fade, and not replaced.

What remains is not a resolution so much as a trained quiet. The piece has spent ten minutes teaching attention to stop reaching past the sound in front of it. It leaves the body almost unused, the ear sharpened, and time slowed into a series of exact arrivals. Its beauty is not softness. It is restraint made audible until restraint becomes enough.
